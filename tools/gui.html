Compile & run

Press Compile to check your code. Files live in my_controllers/ in your project folder.
How it works
Your class gets on_start(brief) once, then on_step(obs) 5 times a second. Return a Command. You never see the rover's true position, only noisy sensors.
Drive
c.drive(speed_m_s, turn_rad_s, brief_.rover) · or set c.wheel_speed_left/right in rad/s. Rovers are slow: ~0.04 m/s.
Sensors in obs
wheel_angle_left/right (encoders), gyro_z, pitch, roll, sun_heading (daytime), landmarks (range+bearing to known boulders), vo_dx/vo_dy (visual odometry), hazards (21 rays ahead), battery_soc, box_temp_c, sun_elevation, relay_in_view, events
Science
Park within 1.5 m of a target and set c.sample_target = id for 4 minutes. Then c.transmit = true during a relay pass. Points count only once data reaches Earth.
Survive
c.sleep_s = 1800 sleeps 30 min (do it at night). If the battery dies, heaters stop and it may freeze.
Debug
c.log = "..." appears in the replay's LOG tab · c.status in the HUD · c.has_estimate, est_x, est_y draws your position estimate · c.planned_path draws your plan.
Full reference
include/aximetry/controller.hpp and docs/writing_a_controller.md. The rover, mission and seed come from the BUILD tab.
